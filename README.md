# indieDev Inc.

**where indie devs, amateurs, and broke-ass builders link up.**

---

hi 👋

We're indieDev Inc.

This is basically a place for people who like making things, messing with code, and seeing how far an idea can go before the code starts fighting back.

We're into software, tools, experiments, weird little projects, and whatever else happens to be worth building.

Nothing here is trying to look like a giant tech company. We're indie devs. Sometimes the idea is solid, sometimes the implementation is questionable, and sometimes something starts as a random thought and somehow becomes an actual project.

That's kind of the point.

## What you'll find here

Mostly projects made by people who enjoy figuring things out themselves.

Android stuff, developer tools, web projects, experiments, utilities, research, and probably a few things that are hard to explain without opening the source.

Some repos may be polished.

Some may be messy.

Some may disappear for a while.

Don't overthink it.

## About the people

We're not here to flex job titles or pretend everything we make is revolutionary.

We just like building stuff.

Some of us are experienced. Some are still learning. Doesn't really matter.

Got an idea? Make it.

Don't know how? Figure it out.

Broke the code? Yeah, that happens.
